# Presentation Guide

Use the poster and the live site together in this order:

1. Explain why investor profiles create different objectives and constraints.
2. Introduce one equation and one intuition for each risk model.
3. Describe the ten-ETF walk-forward backtest, three-year lookback, quarterly
   rebalance, and the no-look-ahead rule.
4. Discuss two or three strong findings for Growth versus Balanced profiles,
   including return, volatility, drawdown, and turnover trade-offs.
5. In the live demo, choose a profile and period, run the time machine, and
   open Strategy Diagnostics to show why allocations differ.

Close with: **The preferred portfolio depends on the investor's objective and
constraints, not just on which model has the highest return.**

The frozen site covers 2016-01-04 through 2025-12-31. If a poster chart uses
2016-2024, call out that presentation-period difference explicitly.
