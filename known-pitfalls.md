# Known Pitfalls

- Do not fit weights on the same period used for evaluation.
- Do not present a two-stock smoke test as portfolio-diversification evidence.
- Do not label profile parameters as active constraints unless the solver actually enforces them.
- Do not compare simple-return and log-return pipelines as though their metrics were identical.
- Do not infer that a visually polished demo is connected to the optimization backend without verifying its data contract.
